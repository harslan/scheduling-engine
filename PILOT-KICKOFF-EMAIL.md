**Subject:** A fairer, repeatable way to do office space (and a link to try it)

Dear Tracey,

I hope Monday went smoothly, and that you and Brenda are settling into 1230.

Watching this week's office shuffle is what pushed me to finish something I have been building. I wanted software that could do this automatically, every semester, and show each person why they got the office they got.

I thought that an office should be earned by a faculty member's teaching schedule, the same way for everyone. You tell it which days you are on campus, and it pairs people whose days never overlap, so two colleagues can share an office and each still has it to themselves when they are in. Trades are allowed, but only when everyone affected agrees. And nothing is official until the faculty vote to adopt the rule.

I didn't fully trust our own tool at first, so we fed it the real teaching schedule, all 120 who taught in person last spring, under invented names, and it matched our hand count to the digit: 61 offices needed, 60 available, short by one, and it named who.

I also want to go first, and now I can do it for real. As I move over to Sargent, I would like to put my office into the system as a shared room anyone can book, and book it like everyone else when I need it.

I wanted you and Brenda to see it for yourselves, so I have set it up for you to try. It is live and private at https://sbs-space-pilot.vercel.app with a dean's-eye sign-in for each of you and a sample faculty login. Passwords are in my separate note.

Everything in it is artificial for now, invented names on a stand-in schedule, so nothing you see is real and nothing you click affects anyone.

Have a look whenever you have a few minutes. I would love to hear what you both think, and I am glad to walk through it together if that would help.

Best,
Hasan
